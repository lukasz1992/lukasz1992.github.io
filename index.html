My Journey with TP-Link xx530v V2

One day, a new TP-Link xx530v V2 router ended up in my hands, and the idea of running OpenWrt on it immediately caught my attention. Based on materials found online, especially from Sirherobrine23, I started preparing the environment and gathering the information needed to work on the device.

After powering the device on and gaining access to the serial console, I discovered something important: the serial interface was not locked, but the V2 hardware was completely different from what I had initially expected. It turned out to be built around the AN7551CT processor, which meant the assumptions I had started with were no longer valid.

Using the x-wrt repositories, I was able to generate an OpenWrt initramfs image to get into the system and access the filesystem, then dump the OEM software. This was a crucial step because it allowed me to work with the actual device instead of guessing based on the wrong hardware revision. In the end, there was no need to look for a workaround like the one described in TP-Link's FAQ just to reach the filesystem.
